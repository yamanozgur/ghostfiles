Ghostfiles — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026 · App: Ghostfiles (com.ghostfiles.app) · Contact: yamanozgur@gmail.com

Ghostfiles is a private vault for photos, videos, documents and notes. It is designed so that we never see your content. This policy explains what the app does with your data.

Summary

  • Your photos, videos, documents and notes are encrypted on your device and stay on your device.
  • We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal data.
  • The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not request internet access.

Data stored on your device

Items you add to the vault (photos, videos, documents, notes, album names) are encrypted with AES-256 and stored only in the app's private storage on your device. Your PIN is never stored; it is used to derive the key that unlocks the vault. If you enable fingerprint or face unlock, the vault key is kept in your device's secure keystore (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain). We have no copy of your PIN, your key or your files, so we cannot recover them if you forget your PIN.

The app's data is excluded from automatic cloud backups by the app. Uninstalling the app deletes the vault.

Permissions

  • Photos and videos: to let you pick items to move into the vault, optionally delete the originals from your gallery, and export items back to your gallery when you ask.
  • Documents: you choose files with your device's system file picker, so the app can only read the files you pick. The originals are not changed or deleted. Copies the file picker caches during import are deleted right after encryption. PDFs are shown inside the app without writing a decrypted copy to storage. If you choose to open a document in another app, a temporary decrypted copy is shared with that app and deleted when you return to Ghostfiles; the other app's own handling of the file is governed by its own policy.
  • Biometrics: only if you turn on fingerprint / face unlock. Biometric data is handled by your device's operating system and is never available to the app.

Purchases

Ghostfiles Pro is sold as a subscription through Google Play or the Apple App Store. Payments are processed by Google or Apple under their own privacy policies. We do not receive your payment details. The app only checks with the store whether your subscription is active.

Children

Ghostfiles is not directed at children and is intended for users aged 18 and over.

Changes

If a future version adds features that handle data differently (for example an optional, end-to-end encrypted cloud backup), we will update this policy and describe the change in the app before it is used.
